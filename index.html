    Summary

    The ASA task force strongly recommends fascial plane blocks in adults to reduce pain or opioid use in the first 24 h after open cardiothoracic, abdominal, retroperitoneal and pelvic surgery and mastectomy, and recommends them after minimally invasive abdominal procedures.     Effectiveness of drug interventions to prevent delirium after surgery in older adults: network meta-analysis

    Luney M et al.

    Summary

    This Bayesian network meta-analysis pooled 158 RCTs (41,084 participants aged 60 or over, 52 drug interventions). Overall delirium risk was 14.5%. Dexmedetomidine (OR 0.46, 95% CrI 0.36 to 0.57) and corticosteroids (OR 0.53, 0.31 to 0.87) were among the drugs linked to lower odds of delirium; dexmedetomidine had the strongest supporting evidence.
