# Anaesthesia Paper Tracker

A one-page web app listing notable anaesthesia papers from the past 12 months:

- **Influential**: practice-relevant papers from NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, BMJ, BJA, Anesthesiology and Anaesthesia, ranked by editorial judgement.
- **Most cited**: the 25 most cited papers by [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org) citation count. The app refreshes these counts from OpenAlex when it opens.
- **Saved**: papers you star (stored only in your browser).

Open it at https://ralbertyn-boop.github.io/anaesthesia-paper-tracker/ and use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" to keep it on your phone.

Summaries are written from each paper's abstract, not copied from it. Figures marked † come from news or society coverage; check them against the original paper. This is a reading aid, not clinical guidance.
